# Hey, I'm Liza 👋

I'm an ML engineer based in Antwerp, Belgium. I build the systems companies use to predict who will buy, who will leave, and what to stock next and I ship them as pipelines that retrain on schedule, not notebooks that collect dust.

Most of my days are a mix of data, models and plumbing: figuring out what a business actually needs to know, building the model that answers it, and making sure it keeps running after I've moved on.

---

### 🔮 What I build

- **Customer models** — segmentation, churn scoring, lifetime-value predictions from transaction data
- **AI agents** — multi-agent fact-checking, document intelligence, workflow automation
- **Production pipelines** — end-to-end ML with retraining, monitoring and drift alerts (MLflow, Airflow, Kafka)
- **Dashboards & reporting** — turning raw data into one clean view that replaces days of manual work (Power BI)
- **Demand forecasting** — weekly predictions that actually update themselves
